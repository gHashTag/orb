Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026
Orb collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry. Your recordings never leave your Mac, because the app is not able to send them anywhere: it ships without the network entitlement, and macOS refuses outbound connections to a sandboxed app that does not declare one.

What Orb handles

Orb records your screen, your camera and your microphone, and it writes what it records to your own disk — by default to ~/Movies/Orb. Projects and exported videos are ordinary files that belong to you. Deleting them deletes them; there is no copy elsewhere.

Orb also reads the position of your pointer while a recording is running, so it can zoom toward what you click. Those coordinates are stored inside the recording's own project file and are used for nothing else.

Permissions are not collection

macOS asks you to grant Orb access to the camera, the microphone and screen recording. Those grants let the app see that material on your machine. They do not send it anywhere, and this policy makes no exception that would let it.

You can withdraw any of them at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Orb keeps working with whatever remains.

No network access at all

This is worth stating precisely, because it is checkable rather than promised. The shipped application declares exactly four entitlements: the App Sandbox, camera, microphone, and read-write access to your Movies folder. It declares neither com.apple.security.network.client nor com.apple.security.network.server. Under the sandbox that is not a policy we follow — it is a capability the operating system withholds.

You can verify it yourself on the copy you downloaded: codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Orb.app.

No tracking, no third parties

Orb contains no advertising identifiers, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters and no third-party libraries that phone home. Nothing about you is shared with anyone, because nothing about you is gathered in the first place.

Buying Orb

If you buy Orb from the Mac App Store, Apple processes the purchase and applies its own privacy policy to it. If you buy it from this site, the payment processor does. In both cases they handle the payment; we never receive or store your card details.

Children

Orb is rated 4+ and is safe for any age, for the same reason as everything above: it gathers no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date. A change that started collecting something would be announced in the app's release notes as well, not only here.
